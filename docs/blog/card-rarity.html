Know your odds
Rarities and pull rates

Every rarity in Welcome to Night City, what's in a pack, how Beta and Retail boxes differ, and your real chances of pulling the card you want.

4 Nov 20265 min readSet 1 · Welcome to Night City

The short version

Every pack has 12 cards: 7 Commons, 3 Uncommons and 2 Rare-or-better. WeirdCo's odds are an Epic about every 4 packs, an Iconic every 18 and a Secret every 24. Beta and Retail share cards and odds but not Iconic art. Nova Rares never come in packs.

01The rarities

The symbol at the bottom of a card sets its rarity. Booster packs hold 140 different cards, 35 per colour, plus 31 Iconic Rares: alternate art of cards already in the set, so they add art, not new cards.

The two starter decks, Embracing Power and The Heist, add 5 exclusive cards each, so Set 1 has 150 mechanically unique cards in all. The starter-deck cards don't appear in booster packs.

02What's in a pack

Every pack has 12 cards: 7 Commons, 3 Uncommons and 2 "hit" slots that are always Rare or better. So every pack has at least two foils.

Rip pack draws at WeirdCo's rates. A God Pack is 12 different Iconic Rares; WeirdCo hasn't published how often they appear, so the sim uses a guess of 1 in 500.

03Pull rates

WeirdCo published these rates in Kickstarter Update #25 (9 April 2026). They're averages across the whole print run, so a single box can do better or worse.

06Nova Rares

Nova Rares never come from booster packs. They're special printings given out as box toppers, prerelease promos, event prizes and convention promos. All but one are versions of cards you can already play from the set or the starter decks. The exception is Rebecca: Having a Moment, which only exists as a promo.
