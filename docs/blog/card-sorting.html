From pile to playset

Learn how to sort and store your Cyberpunk TCG cards, for both your playset and your bulk.

12 Oct 20265 min readSet 1 · Welcome to Night City

01Beta boxes and starter decks

A Beta booster box has 36 packs of 12 cards, so 432 cards per box, plus one box topper. Each pack holds 7 Commons, 3 Uncommons and 2 Rare-or-better slots.

There are also two starter decks, Embracing Power and The Heist, with 43 cards each.

B.3Storage

A binder works best: one card per pocket, every card on show and protected.

Prefer a box? Two alternatives:

Penny sleeves in a storage boxCheapest. Fine for a reserve you don't look at often.
Toploaders in a toploader boxMost protection, most space. Best for the expensive cards.

Binder options

Pocket size

9-pocket

The standard size. Three rows of three, and the easiest to find.

Pocket size

12-pocket

More cards per page, so a slimmer binder for the same set.

Pocket size

4-pocket

Fewer, bigger slots. Good for a showcase of alternate arts and chase cards.

Binder style

Side-loading zip

Cards slide in from the side so they can't fall out, and the zip keeps dust out. Brands include Vault X and Dragon Shield.

Binder style

Ring binder + pages

Add pages as the set grows. Pick D-rings: round rings can press dents into cards near the spine.

Binder style

Toploader binder

Holds cards already in toploaders. Bulky, but the safest binder for valuable cards.

Zip binders ↗ Binder pages ↗ Toploader binders ↗
Sleeved cards?Inner-sleeved cards fit most pockets. Double-sleeved cards often don't, so check the binder says it fits them.

C.1What counts as bulk

Everything left after your playset and reserve set.

Keep a few sparesHold back one or two extra copies of cards people play a lot. They're easy trades, and handy when a friend starts a deck.

C.4Storage

A plain cardboard box with a divider per colour is enough. Bulk doesn't need sleeves.

Skip the rubber bandsThey dent the edges of the top and bottom cards. Stand bulk upright in a box instead.
